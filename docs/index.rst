dandi_acq4
==========

Tools to convert `acq4 <https://www.acq4.org>`_ recordings of single cells (intracellular
recordings, with electrical and optical stimulation) to `NWB <https://www.nwb.org>`_ files,
check them and prepare them for the `DANDI archive <https://dandiarchive.org>`_; to view the NWB
files; and to assemble the slice and cell images into aligned mosaics.
